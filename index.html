Meet Your Dentist

Dr. Jeffrey Hedges, DMD

Born and raised in Southern Indiana, Dr. Hedges is a graduate of Jeffersonville High School. He earned his Bachelor's degree in Health and Human Performance and a Master's degree in Education from the University of Louisville — where he also played quarterback on the UofL Football Team.

Dr. Hedges takes pride in continuing his education in the field of dentistry to stay current and provide his patients with the best care possible. He is passionate about implant dentistry and committed to expanding access to quality dental care.

When he isn't practicing dentistry, Dr. Hedges enjoys spending time with his wife Christy and their three dogs, watching sporting events, and woodworking.

Our Services

Comprehensive Dental Care

From preventive care to advanced restorations, we offer a full range of dental services under one roof.

Cleanings & Exams

Routine preventive care to maintain optimal oral health. Regular cleanings and thorough examinations to catch issues early.

Fillings

High-quality, tooth-colored composite fillings that restore your teeth naturally. Durable and virtually invisible.

Crowns & Bridges

Custom-crafted restorations that protect damaged teeth and bridge gaps left by missing teeth. Built for longevity.

Veneers

Composite and porcelain veneers to transform your smile. Correct discoloration, chips, gaps, and misalignment.

Whitening

Professional teeth whitening to brighten your smile safely and effectively. Noticeable results you'll love.

Oral Surgery

Expert surgical procedures including extractions performed with precision and patient comfort as the priority.

Our Specialty

Dental Implant
Dentistry

Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth — closely mimicking the form and function of a healthy natural tooth. Dr. Hedges has advanced training in implant placement and restoration.

01

Single Tooth Implants

Replace individual missing teeth with a permanent, natural-looking solution that functions just like your original tooth.

02

Multiple Tooth Implants

Restore several missing teeth with strategically placed implants that support bridges or partial restorations.

03

Implant-Retained Dentures

Secure your dentures with implants for a stable, comfortable fit. No more slipping or adhesives.

04

Digital Dentures

Precision-crafted digital dentures using advanced technology for a superior fit and natural appearance.
